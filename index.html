01 — About

About my practice

My first priority is building a comfortable, respectful working relationship — one that lets you begin to focus on the difficult, painful, or complex issues that brought you to counseling. I listen carefully and help guide discussions that support real change: resolving conflict, reducing the weight of anxiety and depressed mood, and learning to manage anger and overwhelm.

My focus is helping clients shift unworkable thinking patterns and coping strategies, drawing on current research in neuroscience, positive neuroplasticity, DBT, and mindfulness-based cognitive therapy. This work supports clients navigating PTSD, high anxiety, phobia, grief, depression, or impulsivity — with the goal of stronger emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and communication.

I love diversity and am genuinely interested in getting to know your unique personality and culture. I am compassionate and engaging in session, and I work to create a safe environment built on respect and non-judgment.

02 — Approach

How I work

Sessions draw on several modalities depending on what's most useful for you, always centered on mindfulness and compassion.

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing

A structured, evidence-based trauma therapy for processing past and current traumatic distress.

Sensorimotor

Body-based trauma work

Helps clients get unstuck from trauma held in the nervous system rather than only in the telling of it.

IFS

Internal Family Systems

Working with the different parts of yourself to build internal understanding and balance.

DBT & MBCT

Skills-based work

Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy and Dialectical Behavior Therapy for emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and communication.

05 — FAQ

Common questions

The things people most often want to know before a first call.

Is Smita Chaddha a licensed therapist in Massachusetts?
Yes — Licensed Mental Health Counselor, Massachusetts license #3829, sole proprietor, NPI 1962504332. You can confirm both independently: the license through the Massachusetts Board of Allied Mental Health Professionals, and the NPI through the CMS NPI Registry. Links are in the "Find & verify me online" section below.
Where does she see clients, and is online available?
The office is at 256 Great Road, Suite 8, Littleton, MA 01460. Sessions are also available online by video, and clients commonly travel from Acton, Groton, Westford and elsewhere in Middlesex County.
What is EMDR, and what does it treat?
EMDR stands for Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing — a structured, evidence-based trauma therapy used to process past and present traumatic distress. Smita is an EMDRIA Certified Therapist™ and an EMDRIA Approved Consultant™, and uses EMDR for PTSD, high anxiety, phobias, grief and depression.
How much does therapy cost, and does she accept insurance?
Fees and coverage vary, so ask when you reach out to verify what applies to you. Blue Cross and Blue Shield, Harvard Pilgrim and Tufts Health Plan are accepted, as well as out-of-network reimbursement. A sliding scale is available for those who may be eligible. Individual sessions are paid by cash or check.
What does the weekly skills group cost and when does it meet?
The adult skills group meets online via Zoom every Wednesday from 11:00 AM to 12:30 PM, at $60 per session. It runs as a basic and advanced psychoeducational group across four modules — mindfulness and distress tolerance, emotional containment, effective communication, and middle path skills. It is intended for clients working alongside individual therapy, and no specific diagnosis is required to benefit.
Is there a free consultation?
Yes — call (978) 808-7269 or email smita@achaddha.com to book a free 15-minute consultation, or to ask about qualifications, training, or what a particular form of therapy will be like.
Which languages does she speak, and who does she work with?
She works in English and Hindi. She sees individuals and groups, and works with teens, adults, and adults aged 65 and over.
