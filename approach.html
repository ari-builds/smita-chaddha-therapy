Modalities

The approaches I use

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing

A structured, evidence-based trauma therapy for processing past and current traumatic distress. Smita is an EMDRIA Certified Therapist™ and an EMDRIA Approved Consultant™, and uses EMDR for PTSD, high anxiety, phobias, grief and depression.

Sensorimotor

Body-based trauma work

Helps clients get unstuck from trauma held in the nervous system rather than only in the telling of it. Smita completed a year-long Sensorimotor Psychotherapy training.

IFS

Internal Family Systems

Working with the different parts of yourself to build internal understanding, self-compassion, and balance.

DBT & MBCT

Skills-based work

Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy and Dialectical Behavior Therapy for emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and communication — including the weekly skills group.
