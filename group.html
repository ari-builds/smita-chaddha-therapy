What to expect

How each week works

A structured hour and a half

The group meets at the same time every week online. Each session pairs a skill lesson with guided practice, so the skills are not just explained but felt and rehearsed before you carry them back into your week.

Skills you can actually use

Mindfulness and distress tolerance give you ground when things feel overwhelming; emotional containment and middle path skills help you steady yourself and communicate more effectively in real relationships.

Safe, respectful, non-judgmental

The same values as individual therapy apply: respect, confidentiality, and no judgment. The group is psychoeducational in focus, and no member is put on the spot to share more than they are comfortable with.
